# Contents Investigation

## Purpose

Determine what is inside the ebook.

Contents describes the reader's experience rather than the identity of the book.

---

## Questions Answered

- Does the book contain a table of contents?
- How many chapters exist?
- Are images embedded?
- Are fonts embedded?
- Is navigation complete?
- Are internal links valid?
- Are required resources missing?
- Does the EPUB package conform to specification?

## Possible Expert Findings

- Table of contents detected.
- Navigation complete.
- Missing table of contents.
- Broken navigation.
- Embedded images detected.
- Missing embedded resources.

---

## Possible Recommendations

- Repair navigation.
- Rebuild table of contents.
- Restore missing resources.

---

## Ownership

Contents owns the structure and reading experience of an ebook.

---

## Out of Scope

Contents never:

- Repairs books.
- Downloads content.
- Evaluates metadata.
- Talks to Scout.

---

## Notes

Contents describes what the reader experiences.

It does not evaluate quality.
