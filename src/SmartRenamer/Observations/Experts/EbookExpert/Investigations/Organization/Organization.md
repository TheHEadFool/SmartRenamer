# Organization Investigation

## Purpose

Determine how the ebook library is organized.

---

## Questions Answered

- Are books grouped by author?
- Are books grouped by series?
- Are books grouped by publisher?
- Are books stored consistently?
- Is the folder structure meaningful?
- Are collections complete?

- Organize by author.
