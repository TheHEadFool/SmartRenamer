# Organization Investigation

## Purpose

Determine how the ebook library is organized.

---

## Questions Answered

- Are books grouped by author?
- Are books grouped by series?
- Are books grouped by publisher?
- Are books stored consistently?
- Is the folder structure meaningful?
- Are collections complete?

## Possible Expert Findings

- Series detected.
- Books grouped by author.
- Inconsistent organization.
- Multiple collections detected.

---

## Possible Recommendations

- Organize by series.
- Organize by author.
- Standardize folder structure.

## Ownership

Organization owns relationships between books.

- Repairs metadata.
- Detects corruption.
