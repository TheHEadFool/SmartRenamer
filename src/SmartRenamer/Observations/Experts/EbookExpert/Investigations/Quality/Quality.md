# Quality Investigation

## Purpose

Evaluate the overall health of the ebook collection.

---

## Questions Answered

- Is metadata complete?
- Are covers present?
- Are ebook files healthy?
- Are required resources missing?
- Are books internally consistent?

---

## Produces

QualityReport

---

## Future Consultants

- Metadata Quality Consultant
- Cover Quality Consultant
- File Integrity Consultant

---

## Future Blocks

- Metadata Validator
- Cover Analyzer
- EPUB Validator

---

## Possible Expert Findings

- High-quality collection.
- Missing covers.
- Poor metadata quality.
- Damaged ebook detected.

---

## Possible Recommendations

- Improve metadata.
- Replace covers.
- Repair damaged books.

---

## Ownership

Quality owns the health assessment of the library.

---

## Out of Scope

Quality never:

- Repairs books.
- Downloads metadata.
- Renames files.
- Talks to Scout.

---

## Notes

Quality measures health.

It does not perform repairs.
